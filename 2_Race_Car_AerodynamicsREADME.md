# Project 2 — Race-Car Aerodynamics

## Objective

Investigate aerodynamic downforce, drag, wings, and diffusers.

## Planned Investigations

- Front-wing and rear-wing geometry
- Angle-of-attack sensitivity
- Diffuser geometry
- Lift and drag coefficients
- Ground-effect sensitivity

## Current Status

Planned. No validated race-car simulation is currently included.

## Future Requirements

A suitable geometry, a verified CFD solver, appropriate boundary
conditions, mesh studies, and comparison with reference data.
